## Deep Dive: Recursive Meta-Loop Engineering — Level 1: Private Score & Fixed Budget

### What it does well
This discipline addresses a core problem in AI agent development: how to ensure that agents improve reliably without exploiting loopholes in reward functions. The approach uses:
- Held-out private scores to prevent overfitting to public metrics
- Fixed budgets to constrain resource consumption during experimentation
- Anti-reward-hacking gates that detect and block exploitative behaviors

It's specifically designed for teams that have already implemented basic agent loops but are struggling with improvement stagnation or unintended consequences from reward hacking.

### What it does NOT do
This is not a pricing strategy guide, nor does it tackle the deeper problem of how to modify an agent's inner loop when failures repeat. It's focused on the immediate discipline of improving while constrained, rather than changing how the system thinks about its own failure modes.

### Who it's for
