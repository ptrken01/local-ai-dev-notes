## A Practical Prompt Template

Here's a runnable prompt template for generating effective email openers:

```prompt
You are an expert newsletter writer. Create 3 opening lines for a newsletter issue about [TOPIC] that:
1. Include a curiosity gap (something intriguing but not misleading)
2. Reference a recent trend or event (within last 60 days)
3. Have a conversational tone
4. Are under 120 characters
5. Include one of these hooks: "You won't believe", "The secret", "This is why"

Current topic: [INSERT TOPIC]
```

Example usage for a newsletter about AI tools:
> You won't believe how many developers are switching to AI coding assistants. The secret? It's not what you think.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I avoid my AI-generated emails being flagged as spam?**
A: Focus on natural language patterns rather than repetitive phrasing. Include genuine curiosity gaps and avoid overused triggers like "free" or "limited time." Test with your actual subscriber list to identify problematic patterns.

**Q: What's the ideal frequency for testing new prompts?**
A: Run 2-3 variations weekly across 100-500 subscribers each. Track open rates and adjust based on statistical significance, not just initial results. This approach scales better than random A/B testing.

**Q: Can I use these prompts with any email platform?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to adapt formatting for each platform. Most platforms support basic HTML templating, so structure your prompts to work within those constraints rather than trying to make them universally compatible.
