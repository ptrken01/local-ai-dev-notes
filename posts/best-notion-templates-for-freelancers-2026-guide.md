# Best Notion Templates for Freelance Client Management (2026)

Three Notion templates are the realistic options for tracking freelance clients, projects, invoices, and delivery in one workspace, and they differ less in features than in what you are handed after checkout. Disclosure first: we sell one of them, so treat this as a vendor comparison with stated prices rather than an independent review. Prices were read from each product page's structured offer data on 2026-09-28.

## How to choose between them

Ask two questions. First, does it give you four linked records — clients, projects, invoices, delivery — or one client list with a notes field? A single database cannot answer "what does this client owe me", because that answer lives in invoices. Second, does it arrive as a Notion page you duplicate, or as data you import? The two formats behave very differently, and [Notion's import documentation](https://www.notion.com/help/import-data-into-notion) is explicit that a CSV import creates a database but cannot create relations, rollups, or formulas.

## Best for the lowest cost with the widest licence: Freelancer Client-CRM, $12

The one to pick if you want the four records separated, a licence that does not block client work, and a file format you own. It is the cheapest of the three and the only one whose licence we can show you. The trade-off is real: you import CSVs, so you wire the three relations yourself.

## Best if you dislike building anything: Notion Client Portal, $13

Pick this if you would rather duplicate a finished Notion page than run an import. At $13 it costs $1 more, and the extra dollar buys the format, not the feature set. We have not used this template, so we make no claim about its depth.

## Best if you also send proposals: Ultimate Freelance Client Portal & Project Proposal, $20

Pick this if proposal generation is part of your problem, because tracking is not. It is $8 above the $12 option and covers a workflow the cheaper templates skip. If you already send proposals elsewhere, that difference is wasted budget.

## What none of them do for you

None of the three create the relations for you. Notion's importer cannot, so whichever you choose, budget five minutes to connect projects to clients, invoices to projects, and delivery to projects. None of them is a substitute for a dedicated accounting tool if you need tax handling or multi-currency invoicing — these are tracking templates, and they are honest about that.

## The 15-minute version

If the decision is about to cost you an hour of reading, buy the $12 one. It is the cheapest, the licence is the least restrictive, and the data is yours in a format no vendor can withdraw.
